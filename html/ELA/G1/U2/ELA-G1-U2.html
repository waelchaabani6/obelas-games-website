How to Play

  • Read the question at the top of the screen and identify the correct object in the picture
  • On a computer (Mouse):
  • Listen to Colors: Move your mouse over the color names to hear them spoken.
  • Select the Target: Click once on the moving target that has the correct color.
  • On a tablet or iPad (touch):
  • Listen to Colors: Tap the color words one time to hear them spoken.
  • Select the Target:Tap twice on the moving target that has the correct color to select the answer.
  • Use the 💡 hint button if you need help.
