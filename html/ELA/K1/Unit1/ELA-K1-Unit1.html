🎮 How to Play

  • Press the green play button to start the game!
  • Listen carefully to the question about the five senses.
  • Click on balloons to pop them and find the correct answer.
  • Use the hint button (💡) if you need help.
  • Click the speaker button (🔊) to hear the question again.
  • Click the stop button (⏹️) to stop all sounds.
