How to Play

  • Press the green play button to start the game!
  • Look carefully at the pictures on the cards.
  • Click two cards to flip them and find a match.
  • Use the hint button (💡) if you need help.
  • Use the speaker button (🔊) to hear the question again.
  • Click the stop button(⏹️) to stop all sounds.
